## Design Context

### Users
Buyers furnishing a new apartment on a budget, and local sellers clearing out furniture. The primary job is choosing one feasible bundle with understandable transport and cost.

### Brand Personality
Simple, approachable, practical. Derived from the creator's explicit Uber Eats simplicity reference and request to prioritize a stable hackathon demo.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light warm ivory, ink-green type, saturated leaf green actions; editorial serif headlines with a humanist sans body. Clear buyer/seller switch, compact category controls, recognizable furniture illustrations, generous space. Route maps explain a selected bundle rather than dominate browsing.

### Design Principles
1. One requirement form leads to complete bundles.
2. Item and delivery prices stay explicit.
3. Transportation and capacity are understandable before checkout.
4. Friendly, concise copy; no internal implementation jargon in the main flow.
5. Keyboard access, visible focus, responsive layouts, reduced motion support.
